# Oryzon

Oryzon is a conceptual vision for the future of spatial computing and digital identity.

## The Problem
Digital portfolios and identities are constrained by 2D rectangles. They lack the spatial memory, atmosphere, and architectural presence of a physical museum or gallery.

## The Solution
We are building interactive, 3D worlds that run seamlessly in the browser at 60 FPS, turning CVs into explorable spaces.

- **Stack**: Next.js, React Three Fiber, Rapier
- **Style**: Brutalism, Monochromatic

*The world is the interface.*
