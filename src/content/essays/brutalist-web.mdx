# The Case for Brutalist Web Architecture

Modern web design has become overly obsessed with rounded corners, soft shadows, and non-committal pastel colors. The internet is losing its architectural edge.

## Structural Honesty
In physical brutalism, concrete is exposed. The structure of the building *is* the aesthetic. On the web, we should expose our structure. 

High contrast. Sharp borders. Monospace fonts.

When you remove the decorative fluff, the content has no choice but to stand on its own merits.

## Performance as an Aesthetic
A brutalist site is fast. By stripping away heavy UI frameworks and relying on stark HTML and minimal CSS, the site loads instantly. Speed is a feature of the aesthetic.
